## 🌟 Key Features in v1.0.0

### 🇮🇳 1. 100% Indian GST Compliance
- **Dual Tax Calculation**: Automatic split into **CGST + SGST** (intra-state) or **IGST** (inter-state) based on Buyer/Seller Place of Supply (POS).
- **GST Returns Ready**: Full support for **GSTR-1** (B2B, B2C Large, B2C Small), **GSTR-3B**, and **HSN/SAC Summaries**.
- **Professional Tax Invoices**: Print-ready GST invoices formatted according to Indian tax regulations (with QR code area, bank details, reverse charge, and numbers converted to words in Lakhs/Crores).
- **15-Digit GSTIN Validation**: Built-in format verification for state codes, PAN, entity numbers, and checksums.

### 💼 2. Tailored for IT & CCTV Service Businesses
- **Hardware & Peripherals**: Pre-configured support for Laptops, Desktops, CCTV Cameras (IP/Dome/Bullet), NVRs, Network Switches, Firewalls, Storage & UPS.
- **Service & AMC Support**: Built-in 6-digit SAC codes for Annual Maintenance Contracts (`998314`), CCTV Installation (`998714`), Network Setup (`998313`), and On-Site Support (`998731`).

### 📦 3. Real-Time Inventory & Stock Management
- **Stock Tracking**: Auto-adjusts stock upon invoice generation and purchase entries.
- **Low Stock Alerts**: Configurable reorder levels with automatic alert flags and purchase recommendation quantities.
- **Service Items Handling**: Unlimited non-depleting stock mode for AMC and labor charges.

### 👥 4. Customer & Supplier CRM + Aging Analysis
- **Contact Database**: Complete profiles with GSTIN, billing/shipping address, PAN, and contact details.
- **Receivables & Payables**: Visual payment status tracking (Paid, Unpaid, Overdue) with aging analysis buckets (0–30, 31–60, 60+ days).

### 🤖 5. Built-in AI Business Assistant
- **Business Health Score**: Plain-language financial diagnostics and profit margin analysis.
- **Automated Reorder Planning**: Calculates order quantities based on consumption velocity.
- **Sales & Revenue Forecasting**: 6-month historical trend analysis.
- **GST Compliance Audit**: Flags missing HSN codes or invalid customer GSTINs.

## 💻 Quick Start Instructions (Windows)

1. Download **`ShopPulse-v1.0.0-windows-x64.zip`** from the release assets.
2. Right-click the `.zip` file and select **Extract All...** to any folder (e.g. `C:\ShopPulse` or `Desktop`).
3. Open the extracted folder and double-click **`ShopPulse.exe`** to launch.
4. *(Optional)* Right-click `ShopPulse.exe` → **Send to** → **Desktop (create shortcut)** for easy access.
